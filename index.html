Today's routine

0 of 5 done

  1. do

    Breathe

    2 min

    Stand tall with loose shoulders. Breathe in through your nose for four counts so your belly and lower ribs expand, not your chest. Then let it out on a steady "sss" for as long as it stays even. Do three rounds.

    Tap start when you're ready 0.0 s

    Most beginners start around 10 to 20 seconds. Steady matters more than long.

  2. re

    Warm up

    2 min

    Do lip trills: close your lips loosely and blow so they flutter like a motorboat, then add your voice and slide up and back down with the tone. If trills won't happen yet, hum with your lips closed instead. Five slides.

  3. mi

    Match the note

    3 min

    Tap a note, listen, then sing it back on "ah" and hold it for three seconds. If it sounds wavy against the tone, slide slowly up or down until the waves smooth out. That smooth sound means you've matched it.

  4. fa

    Five-note scale

    3 min

    Play the pattern, then sing it back on "ah": do re mi fa sol fa mi re do. Move up a half step and repeat. Stop going higher as soon as your throat feels tight; that's today's top.

    C3
  5. sol

    Cool down

    1 min

    Hum quietly with your lips closed and slide down from the middle of your range to your lowest easy note. Keep it soft and lazy. Three slides, then you're done for today.

Ground rules

  • Sing at talking volume. Loud doesn't help while you're learning.
  • If it hurts or your voice goes hoarse, stop and rest a day. Hoarse for more than two weeks? Get it checked by a doctor.
  • Sip water, and hum or swallow instead of clearing your throat.
  • Ten minutes every day beats an hour once a week.
  • No sound on iPhone? Turn off silent mode.
